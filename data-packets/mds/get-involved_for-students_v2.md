## Step Into the Real World of Climate Action

Climate work happens where science, technology, finance, policy,
and community action meet. At MEF, you work at that crossroads —
beyond classroom learning, inside the working ecosystem.

You may work alongside:

- Climate projects
- Startups & accelerators
- Researchers & professionals
- Investors & mentors
- Corporates & CSR teams
- Policy & institutional partners

## What Can You Do?

### Join an Internship
Apply independently or through your university. Work on research,
content, programmes, community, or partnerships.

### Work on Climate Projects
Support field initiatives, reports, startup programmes, and campaigns.

### Research & Publish
Contribute to reports, case studies, and sector research.

### Attend Events & Webinars
Meet founders, investors, policymakers, and researchers.

### Learn from Mentors
Guidance from people working across climate tech, finance,
policy, and research.

### Take Leadership
Lead a student chapter, project, event, or working group.

## What Will You Gain?

Recognition for internships, projects, and contributions.

Selected internships and project roles include a stipend.

Evidence of real work beyond classroom assignments.

A visible record of your projects, research, publications,
certificates, and leadership.

1. Join MEF
2. Attend an orientation or webinar

4. Work on a project or internship
5. Contribute research, content, or fieldwork
6. Earn recognition and certificates
7. Build your climate profile — and access mentors, networks,
   and career opportunities
