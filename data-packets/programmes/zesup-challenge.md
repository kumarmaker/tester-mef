The ZeSUP Challenge (Zero Single-Use Plastic Challenge) is a targeted climate-tech accelerator program focused entirely on eliminating single-use plastic waste within the Food & Beverage (F&B) industry.

The program was launched as a collaborative initiative by the sustainability-focused fund Climate Angels, the GoMassive Earth Network (the investment arm of the Massive Earth Foundation), and The Incubation Network.

## The F&B Plastic Crisis Focus

Packaging in the F&B sector—particularly delivery, drive-throughs, takeaways, and food supply lines—is dominated by hard-to-recycle materials like polystyrene, flexibles, and films. Because the restaurant sector relies heavily on fixed supply chains, it is historically slow to adopt alternative, circular packaging. The ZeSUP Challenge scouts early-stage startups that offer viable alternatives to traditional plastics or invent innovative reuse and refill business models.

## Funding & Ecosystem Perks

- **The Funding Pool:** Selected startups gain access to a dedicated project and venture funding pool of USD $500,000.
- **Commercial Pilots:** Startups don't just receive capital; they secure direct commercial partnerships to deploy paid pilots with major industry partners like Rebel Foods (the world's largest internet restaurant company behind brands like Faasos and Behrouz Biryani).
- **Scientific Environmental Study:** The data and insights derived from the startup pilots are used to formulate a broader academic study. This study outlines carbon savings and evaluates the true environmental impact of plastic replacements versus virgin plastic production.

## Global Backing & Governance

While GoMassive handles the local accelerator execution and alignment in India, the program features high-level international backing:

- **Funded By:** The PREVENT Waste Alliance — an initiative of the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) — alongside the ECCA Family Foundation.
- **Programmatic Framework:** It operates as part of the broader international Innovate & PREVENT program.
