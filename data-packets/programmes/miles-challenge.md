The MILES Challenge (M+ Innovation Lab for Energy and Sustainability) is a prominent clean energy incubation and acceleration program. It was launched by Amplus Solar—Asia's leading distributed energy company and a member of the PETRONAS Group—in strategic partnership with the GoMassive Earth Network (the investment arm of the Massive Earth Foundation).

The primary purpose of the challenge is to track down, mentor, and fund early-stage startups that possess ready-to-deploy clean energy solutions in India.

## Core Focus Sectors

The program targets innovation across four main technical pillars:

- **Distributed Solar:** Rooftop systems, off-grid architectures, and localized generation.
- **Electric Mobility:** Infrastructure, EV fleet management, and clean transit alternatives.
- **Battery Storage:** Advanced energy storage systems (BESS) and management systems.
- **IoT Applications in Energy:** Smart grid management, AI energy analytics, and digital asset tracking.

## Funding & Ecosystem Incentives

- **Seed Capital:** The winner of the challenge stands to receive up to $100,000 in seed capital directly from Amplus Solar to scale their commercial operations.
- **Pilot Deployment Opportunities:** Startups retain 100% of their intellectual property (IP) while gaining the chance to run paid pilots across Amplus Solar's massive corporate client portfolio.
- **High-Level Jury Exposure:** Startups pitch directly to an elite panel composed of clean energy experts from institutions like the World Bank, UNEP, and IIM Ahmedabad.
- **Process Governance:** The entire structural flow, framework vetting, and financial tracking of the accelerator are supported by Ernst & Young (EY) acting as official process advisors.

## Strategic Context within MEF

The MILES Challenge sits alongside programs like AgroXLerate and Project SAFFAL under the Massive Earth Foundation / GoMassive ecosystem. While MEF's other initiatives lean toward carbon removal, agriculture, and gender-lens financing, MILES remains strictly dedicated to building an industrial-grade green tech backbone via energy efficiency and clean mobility tech.
