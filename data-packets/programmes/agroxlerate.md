AgroXLerate is a premier flagship climate-tech accelerator program specifically designed to scale early-stage Agri-Tech startups building sustainable, climate-resilient solutions for India's agricultural sector.

The equity-free and fee-free program is co-hosted by the Massive Earth Foundation (MEF), Pantnagar Capital, Climate Angels, and the GoMassive Earth Network.

## Program Mission & Purpose

Agriculture accounts for immense climate vulnerability and carbon footprint. AgroXLerate was built to close the tech gap in farming by turning sustainable ideas into investor-ready business models. It targets issues spanning from food security to climate adaptation.

## Core Pillars & Structure

- **Duration:** An intensive 10 to 12-week online bootcamp.
- **Frequency:** Structured around two high-impact digital sessions every week.
- **Capacity Building:** Mentorship focuses heavily on business refinement, navigating the regulatory environment, and technical scaling.
- **The Culmination:** Concludes with an in-person Demo Day where top-performing startups pitch directly to active venture capitals and climate investors.

## Key Focus Sectors

The accelerator splits its cohort intake across six technical sub-sectors:

1. **PhytoGenix:** Agricultural biotechnology innovations.
2. **CropCapital:** Agri-finance, financial technologies, and crop yield optimization.
3. **AgriStream:** Advanced hardware and software for upstream agricultural cultivation.
4. **GrainFlow:** Supply chain logistics, cold-chain, and post-harvest management technologies.
5. **PrecisionGrow:** Smart farming, AI-powered pest detection, and precision agri-tech.
6. **ClimateCrop:** Dedicated technologies resolving intersections between regional climate changes and food security.

## Strategic Network

Participating startups interact with high-profile industry veterans. AgroXLerate added Hemendra Mathur (Venture Partner at Bharat Innovation Fund and Co-founder of ThinkAg) as an ecosystem mentor to help companies secure institutional funding.
