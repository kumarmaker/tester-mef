The Agra Innovation Lab (AIL)—formally known as the Agra Innovation Lab for Prevention of Plastic Leakage into the Environment—is a targeted municipal-level acceleration and pilot testing platform.

It was established in 2021 by the Massive Earth Foundation (MEF) and the GoMassive Earth Network in partnership with the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), The Incubation Network (TIN), and the Agra District Administration. The program acts as a direct implementation tool for Agra's city-wide Clean Air Action Plan.

## Purpose & Core Mission

The city of Agra generates immense amounts of municipal plastic waste that poses localized environmental threats. This waste routinely risks polluting critical air sheds (via open burning) and leaking directly into regional soil and water networks—endangering iconic global heritage sites like the Taj Mahal.

The Agra Innovation Lab crowdsources, accelerates, and fund-matches early-stage Indian climate-tech startups capable of deploying immediate, real-world solutions directly inside Agra's municipal grid.

## Strategic Technical Pillars

The framework focuses exclusively on driving a circular economy by resolving four primary gaps in the city's waste value chain:

- **Waste Identification:** Leveraging AI and machine learning to map out and classify waste accumulation points.
- **Mechanized Collection & Transport:** Upgrading physical collection routines to stop waste from spilling or leaking en route.
- **Advanced Sorting:** Deploying smart sorting mechanisms to isolate high-value recyclables.
- **Unique Recycling Processes:** Processing hard-to-recycle materials into reusable products.

## Notable Ecosystem Partners

- **The Incubation Network & Alliance to End Plastic Waste:** Provides critical funding infrastructure and strategic alignment across South Asia.
- **Social Alpha (backed by Tata Trusts):** Integrates tech-driven social impact frameworks while focusing on improving the livelihoods of Agra's informal waste-picking sector.
- **AGNIi (Invest India):** Collaborates closely as a tech scouting and strategic outreach partner to source elite hardware founders.

## Notable Shortlisted Startups

**ClearBot:** Developed zero-emission robotic boats powered by computer vision. These autonomous vessels isolate, classify, and clear marine waste and plastic pollution from inland water bodies up to 4x more efficiently than standard municipal operations.

## Integration with Long-Term Municipal Strategy

The infrastructure and insights curated by the lab paved the way for permanent municipal upgrades. Agra transformed its old dumping grounds into a comprehensive Integrated Waste Management City, launching an industrial-scale Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) and a plastic waste processing plant. Today, plastic collected across Agra is mechanically recycled directly into heavy-duty agricultural water pipes sold affordably to local farmers.
