## Programme Identity

- **Full name:** South Asia Finance Facility for Acceleration and Leverage
- **Short name:** SAFFAL
- **Type:** Climate accelerator + blended-finance platform
- **Focus:** Women-led and women-driven SMEs at the intersection of climate action and gender equity
- **Region:** South Asia (India, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka)
- **Delivered under:** EmPower: Women for Climate-Resilient Societies Phase 2
- **Partners:** UNEP, UN Women
- **Funder:** Ministry of Sweden
- **Website:** https://saffal.asia/

## Key Numbers

- 364 applications from 5 South Asian countries in first cohort
- $6M blended-finance facility (grant, debt, equity, catalytic capital)
- 12-week cohort-based bootcamp
- First cohort timeline: applications opened August 2025 → 3-month online bootcamp from December 2025 → Demo Day + capital close early 2026

## Programme Structure

**Three pillars:**
1. **Accelerator** — 12-week cohort bootcamp, intensive mentor evaluation, investor Demo Day
2. **Finance Facility** — $6M blended-finance facility; handles entity setup, legal mechanics, institutional-grade governance; capital structured as grant, debt, equity, and catalytic instruments
3. **Impact Governance** — financial compliance enforcement, third-party impact tracking against IRIS+ metrics, board-ready reporting for DFIs

**Additional support:** Financial modelling, valuation, go-to-market technical assistance. Platform credits from Google Cloud, Microsoft, and HubSpot.

## Design Principles

- **Climate-first, not startup-first:** Mentorship built around climate use-cases, asset-heavy business models, and the regulatory landscapes these ventures actually face
- **Gender-lens by design:** Works only with women-led and women-driven enterprises
- **Capital gap focus:** Backs businesses routinely bypassed by commercial debt, DFIs, and traditional equity

## Target Sectors

Carbon removal, renewable energy and efficiency, fuel switching, waste management, agri-tech.

## Eligibility

Registered, women-led ventures in India, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka.

## Policy Context

- Delivery mechanism under EmPower Phase 2 (UNEP + UN Women)
- Responds to India NDC 3.0 requirement for gender-responsive climate finance mechanisms
- Aligned to Paris Agreement finance mobilisation mandate and Baku-to-Belém Roadmap
